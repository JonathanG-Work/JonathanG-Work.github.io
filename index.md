Independent research consultancy specialising in survey methodology, data analysis, and stakeholder engagement across the public, not-for-profit, and private sectors.


## Expertise

We work across the full research lifecycle, from study design and fieldwork through to analysis, visualisation, and reporting. Our technical toolkit includes R / RStudio for statistical modelling and geographic analysis, alongside enterprise survey platforms. Outputs are tailored to the audience and context, designed to meet a plethora of needs, be they written reports, interactive tools, self-contained documents, or online formats.

We have particular depth in longitudinal survey design, community and stakeholder profiling, and translating complex data into accessible insight for non-specialist audiences.


## Recent Projects

**IPSASB International Public Sector Financial Accountability Index 2025**  
Multilingual stakeholder engagement survey (Arabic, English, French, Spanish) combined with R-based geographic analysis, supporting a joint IFAC / CIPFA publication tracking accrual accounting adoption by governments worldwide. [Read the report](https://www.ifac.org/news-events/2025-09/global-public-sector-shift-accrual-accounting-steadies)

**Distance Enquiry Services Survey 2023 & 2025**  
Remote and virtual service usage across UK archive services, the seventh wave of a longitudinal study running since 2013, covering 70 repositories and approaching 2,000 responses across the full dataset. Delivered for the Archives and Records Association (ARA).

**Survey of Visitors to UK Archives 2024**  
In-person visitor experience and physical footfall across UK archive services. A methodologically distinct companion to the Distance Enquiry series, capturing the experience of users who come to the archive rather than the archive coming to them. Delivered for ARA.

Reports from both ARA survey series are available on the [ARA resources page](https://www.archives.org.uk/resources#surveys). The 2025 Distance Enquiry Services Survey report is currently with the steering group and will be available there shortly.

**Private Sector Engagement, Security Sector**  
Three-phase research programme for a private sector security organisation, comprising desk research and literature review, an online survey, and a training needs analysis to support workforce development planning.
